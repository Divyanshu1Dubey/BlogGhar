# BMI Calculator India 2026: Complete Guide to Body Mass Index

## What is BMI (Body Mass Index)?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple calculation using a person's height and weight. The formula is:

**BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²**

For example, if you weigh 70 kg and your height is 1.75 meters, your BMI would be:
70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.86

## How to Calculate BMI at Home

### Using Metric System
```
BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
```

### Using Imperial System (for NRIs)
```
BMI = [Weight (lbs) / Height (in)²] × 703
```

### Example Calculations

**Example 1:**
- Weight: 65 kg
- Height: 1.60 m
- BMI: 65 ÷ (1.60 × 1.60) = 25.4 → Overweight

# Percentage Calculator: Complete Guide for 2026

## What is Percentage?

The term "percent" comes from the Latin "per centum" meaning "by a hundred." A percentage is a fraction or ratio expressed as a number out of 100.

**Symbol: %**

---

## Types of Percentage Calculations

### 1. Basic Percentage
**What is X% of Y?**

Formula: (X/100) × Y

Example: What is 20% of 500?
= (20/100) × 500 = 100

### 2. Percentage Of
**X is what percent of Y?**

Formula: (X/Y) × 100

Example: 25 is what percent of 200?
= (25/200) × 100 = 12.5%

### 3. Percentage Change
**Increase/Decrease from X to Y**

Formula: ((Y - X)/X) × 100

Example: Price increased from ₹100 to ₹150
= ((150-100)/100) × 100 = 50% increase

# EMI Calculator India 2026: Complete Guide

## What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed payment a borrower makes to a lender on a specified date each month. It includes both principal and interest.

---

## EMI Calculator

**[Insert EMI Calculator Widget Here]**

Enter loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to calculate your EMI instantly!

---

## EMI Formula

### Standard Formula
```
EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:
P = Principal loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate/12/100)
n = Loan tenure in months
```

### Example Calculation
- Loan Amount: ₹10,00,000
- Interest Rate: 8.5% p.a.
- Tenure: 20 years (240 months)

```
r = 8.5/(12×100) = 0.007083
EMI = [10,00,000 × 0.007083 × (1.007083)^240] / [(1.007083)^240 - 1]
EMI = ₹8,636 per month
```

## Rules for Roman Numerals

### Rule 1: Addition
When a smaller numeral follows a larger one, add them.
- VI = 5 + 1 = 6
- XV = 10 + 5 = 15
- LXX = 50 + 10 + 10 = 70

### Rule 2: Subtraction
When a smaller numeral precedes a larger one, subtract.
- IV = 5 - 1 = 4
- IX = 10 - 1 = 9
- XL = 50 - 10 = 40
- XC = 100 - 10 = 90

### Rule 3: Repetition
A numeral can be repeated up to 3 times for addition.
- III = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3
- XXX = 10 + 10 + 10 = 30
- CCC = 100 + 100 + 100 = 300

### Rule 4: No Subtraction for 5s
- V, L, D cannot be subtracted
- Write 95 as XCV (100 - 10 + 5), not VC

## Minesweeper Strategy: Essential Tips

### Tip 1: First Click is Always Safe
The first click in modern Minesweeper is guaranteed to be safe. It never reveals a mine or a number adjacent to a mine.

### Tip 2: The 1-2-3 Rule
```
1 2 1
2 ? 2
1 2 1
```
If you see this pattern, the middle square (?) is NOT a mine. The numbers add up to 8 adjacent squares with exactly 8 mines.

### Tip 3: The 1-1 Rule
```
1 1 ?
```
If two "1"s share a common empty square, that square is NOT a mine.

### Tip 4: Edge Detection
Numbers on edges have fewer adjacent squares, making deductions easier:
- Corner: 3 adjacent squares
- Edge: 5 adjacent squares
- Center: 8 adjacent squares

### Tip 5: Flag Everything You're Sure Of
Don't just guess - flag when you're certain. Unnecessary flags block progress.

# Tic Tac Toe Strategy: How to Win Every Time

## What is Tic Tac Toe?

Tic Tac Toe (also known as Noughts and Crosses or X's and O's) is a classic two-player game where players take turns marking spaces in a 3×3 grid. The player who succeeds in placing three of their marks in a diagonal, horizontal, or vertical row is the winner.

**Origin:** Ancient Egypt (1300 BC)
**Other Names:** Noughts and Crosses, X's and O's
**Grid:** 3×3 (9 squares)

---

## How to Play Tic Tac Toe

### Rules
1. Player X goes first
2. Players alternate turns
3. Place mark in any empty square
4. First to 3 in a row wins
5. If all squares filled with no winner, it's a draw

### Winning Combinations
- Horizontal: Top, Middle, Bottom rows
- Vertical: Left, Middle, Right columns
- Diagonal: Top-left to bottom-right, Top-right to bottom-left

---

## Tic Tac Toe Strategy: Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Always Take the Center
The center square is involved in 4 possible winning lines (middle row, middle column, both diagonals). It's the most strategic position.

### Tip 2: Take Corners When Center is Taken
Corners are involved in 3 winning lines each. Better than edges.

### Tip 3: Block Opponent's Winning Moves
Always check if opponent has two in a row and block immediately.

### Tip 4: Create a Fork
A fork is when you have two ways to win on your next turn. Opponent can only block one.

### Tip 5: Play the Optimal Strategy
With perfect play, Tic Tac Toe always ends in a draw. Here's the algorithm:

**If you're X (first player):**
1. Take center (if available)
2. Take corner (if center taken)
3. Block opponent's threats
4. Create fork opportunity

**If you're O (second player):**
1. Take center (if X didn't)
2. Take corner opposite X's first move
3. Block all threats
4. Force a draw

- - -    X - -    - - X

# UPI Safety Guide: How to Avoid Common Payment Scams in India

UPI makes everyday payments quick, but speed can also make mistakes expensive. The safest habit is to pause before approving a transaction and remember that receiving money never requires entering your UPI PIN.

## Understand the direction of money

Your UPI PIN authorizes a payment from your account. It is not needed to receive a refund, accept a salary, or collect a prize. A QR code is generally used to pay, not to receive money. Read the name and amount shown by your app before confirming.

## Treat urgency as a warning sign

Scammers may claim to be delivery agents, bank staff, employers, or relatives. Do not install screen-sharing applications at their request, reveal an OTP or PIN, or move a conversation to an untrusted link. Call the organization using a number from its official website or app.

## If a payment goes wrong

Immediately report the transaction in your UPI app and contact your bank through its official support channel. Save the transaction ID, time, amount, phone number, and screenshots. Report financial cybercrime promptly through the official Indian cybercrime portal or helpline, and never pay someone who promises to recover funds.

## Make your account harder to misuse

Use a strong device lock, update the operating system, enable transaction alerts, and keep banking apps behind a separate app lock where available. Review mandates and autopay instructions regularly. Set a lower daily limit if your bank supports it and keep only the necessary balance in frequently used accounts.

Good payment security is mostly a set of small pauses: verify the recipient, verify the amount, and authorize only what you intended.

# How to Build a Better Exam Study Plan Without Burning Out

A strong study plan is not a timetable packed from morning to night. It is a repeatable system that helps you remember important ideas and find weak areas early.

## Begin with a diagnostic

List every subject and divide each syllabus into small topics. Attempt a short set of questions before studying so you can identify what you know, what you partly know, and what is unfamiliar. This prevents spending most of your time rereading comfortable chapters.

## Use active recall

Close the book and explain a concept from memory. Write answers to practice questions, draw a process, or teach the idea aloud. Then compare your attempt with the source and correct it. Retrieval feels harder than rereading, but it gives you better information about what you can actually reproduce in an exam.

## Space your revision

Review a topic on the same day, a few days later, and again after a longer gap. Keep a mistake notebook with the question, your error, and the corrected method. Revisit mistakes more often than questions you already solve reliably.

## Plan energy as well as time

Schedule difficult work when you are most alert. Alternate demanding problem-solving with lighter review, take short movement breaks, and protect sleep before the final week. A plan that leaves no recovery time usually collapses after a few days.

At the end of each week, measure completed practice and unresolved mistakes rather than counting hours alone. Adjust the next week based on evidence.

# Home Solar in India: What to Check Before Installing Rooftop Panels

Rooftop solar can reduce grid electricity use, but the right system depends on your consumption, roof, local rules, and budget. Treat it as a long-term infrastructure decision rather than an impulse purchase.

## Start with your bills

Review at least twelve months of electricity bills and note seasonal changes. A vendor should size the system from measured consumption and usable roof area, not from a generic package. Ask how much shade appears during different parts of the day.

## Inspect the roof and electrical system

The roof should be structurally sound and accessible for cleaning and maintenance. Check the age of waterproofing, cable routes, earthing, and the location of the inverter. Keep clear access for future repairs and avoid placing equipment where water collects.

## Confirm approvals and warranty

Ask the local distribution company about net-metering or other connection requirements. Get written details for panel performance, inverter warranty, installation workmanship, monitoring, maintenance, and what happens if a component fails. Compare total installed cost rather than only the panel price.

## Think about safety and expectations

Use qualified installers, proper protection devices, and compliant wiring. Solar does not guarantee zero bills: fixed charges, cloudy periods, export rules, and nighttime consumption still matter. Request a realistic generation estimate for your location and keep copies of every document.

Official information changes by state and scheme, so verify current requirements with the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy and your electricity distributor before paying an advance.
