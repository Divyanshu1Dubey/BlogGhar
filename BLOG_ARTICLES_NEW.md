## 1. TOURIST VISA / e-VISA (MOST COMMON)

### What Is It?

The Tourist Visa is designed for foreign nationals visiting India for leisure, sightseeing, short-term visits to family, and conferences. The **e-Visa** is the simplified digital version available to citizens of 189 eligible countries as of 2026.

### Eligibility Requirements

- Must be a citizen of one of 189 eligible countries (list updated regularly on inda.gov.in)
- Minimum 6 months validity in passport from date of application
- No prior criminal convictions
- Clean immigration record

**Countries NOT eligible for e-Visa (as of 2026):**
Pakistan, Afghanistan, Sudan, Syria, Iran, Yemen, North Korea, Palestine, Maldives, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Sri Lanka (partial restrictions may apply—verify at official portal)

### Duration & Validity

**e-Visa Options:**
- **30-day visa:** Single entry, valid for 30 days from issue date
- **1-year visa:** Multiple entries, valid for 1 year, each stay max 90 days
- **5-year visa:** Multiple entries, valid for 5 years, each stay max 90 days

**Important note:** Validity ≠ permission to stay. You must exit India before the validity expires.

### Application Process & Steps

**Step 1: Online Application (15 minutes)**
1. Visit https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/
2. Select visa type and country
3. Fill form with passport details, contact information, travel plans
4. Upload documents:
   - Scanned passport bio page (color scan, readable)
   - Recent passport-size photo (JPG/PDF, specific dimensions)
5. Pay application fee online (Visa/Mastercard/American Express accepted)

**Step 2: Receive Approval Email**
- Processing time: Typically 48-72 hours (can be faster)
- Approved e-Visa will be emailed as PDF
- Print the e-Visa (important: have physical or digital copy available)

**Step 3: Arrive in India**
- Present printedApprovals/digital e-Visa at airport immigration
- Have following ready:
  - Passport
  - Printed e-Visa
  - Return ticket
  - Proof of accommodation (hotel booking, friend's address)
  - Evidence of funds (showing ability to support stay)

| 30-day e-Visa | ₹1,050 | $12.50 | £10.50 | €11.50 |

*Rates fluctuate based on exchange rates and government updates*

### Common Issues & Solutions

## 2. STUDENT VISA

### What Is It?

The Student Visa is for international students enrolled in full-time educational programs at recognized Indian institutions (colleges, universities, technical institutes).

### Eligibility

- Admission letter from recognized Indian educational institution
- Proof of financial capability to support studies + living expenses
- Clean medical and criminal record
- No prior visa violations in India
- Full-time enrollment status

### Duration

- Granted for the duration of the course + 6-12 months buffer
- Typically 1-5 years
- Can be renewed with proof of continued enrollment

### Application Process

**Step 1: Get Admission Letter**
- Secure admission to an Indian institution
- Institution will issue official admission letter mentioning:
  - Course duration
  - Start date
  - Fees
  - Institution's approval

**Step 2: Apply Through Visa Application Center**
- Visit Indian embassy/consulate in your country OR
- Apply through authorized VAC (Visa Application Center)
- Required documents:
  - Passport (6+ months validity)
  - Admission letter (original + copy)
  - Financial documents (bank statements, sponsor letter)
  - Medical certificate
  - Form visa application form

**Step 3: Interview (if required)**
- Some missions conduct interviews
- Prepare to discuss:
  - Why this course and institution?
  - Career goals
  - Financial arrangements
  - Return plans after studies

**Step 4: Processing**
- Processing time: 4-6 weeks
- Approved visa stamped in passport

### Fees (2026)

- Approximate range: ₹5,000-₹15,000 ($60-$180 USD)
- Varies by country and embassy
- Check specific embassy website for exact fees

### Important Rules While in India

**You CAN:**
- Study full-time at enrolled institution
- Work on campus (max 20 hours/week during semester)
- Travel within India during vacations
- Change institutions (requires fresh visa application if changing course type)

**You CANNOT:**
- Work outside campus
- Engage in self-employment
- Change course without permission
- Travel outside India without re-entry permit
- Overstay (serious immigration violation)

### Post-Graduation Options (2026)

**Option 1: Optional Practical Training (OPT-equivalent)**
- Not formally named "OPT" but Indian institutions offer internships
- Typically 6-12 months after graduation
- Internship at Indian or international company

**Option 2: Change to Employment Visa**
- Employer must sponsor
- Processing time: 4-6 weeks
- Requires proof of job offer

**Option 3: Return Home or Move to Another Country**
- You have 30-60 days to leave after course completion

## 3. EMPLOYMENT VISA (WORK VISA)

### What Is It?

The Employment Visa (also called Work Visa or E-2 Visa) is for foreign professionals employed by Indian companies or their subsidiaries.

### Eligibility

- Job offer letter from Indian company
- Company registration and financial proof
- Specific skill requirement not easily available in India
- Minimum monthly salary (varies, typically ₹50,000+)
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional qualification
- Previous experience relevant to role

### Duration

- Initially granted for 1 year
- Can be renewed for up to 3 years total
- Extensions possible with employer sponsorship

### Application Process

**Step 1: Company Processes Application**
- Your employer obtains:
  - Registration certificate
  - Authority to employ foreigners
  - No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Ministry of External Affairs if needed
- Takes 2-4 weeks

**Step 2: You Apply at Embassy/VAC**
- Submit documents:
  - Passport
  - Job offer letter (details role, salary, duration)
  - Company documents (registration, financial statements)
  - Educational certificates
  - Medical report
  - Police clearance
  - Visa application form

**Step 3: Interview & Processing**
- Interview may be conducted
- Processing time: 2-4 weeks

**Step 4: Visa Grant**
- Stamped in passport
- You can then join employment in India

### Fees (2026)

- Approximate range: ₹6,000-₹20,000 ($72-$240 USD)
- Varies by country and embassy

### Dependent/Family Members

- Spouse and children (under 21) can apply for **Dependent Visas**
- Dependent visa attached to your employment visa
- Duration matches your employment visa
- Dependents cannot work but can study

### Important Rules

- Visa is tied to specific employer
- Changing jobs requires:
  - New visa application, OR
  - Employer visa transfer process
- Overstaying or working for unauthorized employer is serious violation
- Subject to workplace regulations and labor laws

## 4. BUSINESS VISA

### What Is It?

For foreign entrepreneurs, business owners, consultants, or those attending business meetings in India. Can be used for:
- Trade and business meetings
- Attending conferences
- Exploring business opportunities
- Installing machinery/equipment
- Consulting work

### Eligibility

- Business registration/trade license in your home country, OR
- Letter from Indian business partner/collaborator, OR
- Conference/seminar registration
- Proof of business experience

### Duration

- 1 year to 5 years (multiple entry)
- Each stay typically max 180 days

### Application Process

**Step 1: Prepare Business Documentation**
- Business registration certificate from home country
- Invitation letter from Indian business partner, OR
- Conference registration
- Business profile/past work samples
- Financial documents

**Step 2: Apply at Embassy/VAC**
- Submit passport + documents
- Interview may be conducted

**Step 3: Processing**
- Time: 2-4 weeks
- Visa stamped in passport

### Fees (2026)

- Approximate range: ₹12,000-₹25,000 ($144-$300 USD)
- More expensive than tourist/student visas

## 5. MEDICAL VISA

### What Is It?

For foreign nationals seeking medical treatment at Indian hospitals and their accompanying attendants.

### Eligibility

- Medical recommendation from recognized Indian hospital
- Proof of financial capability for treatment
- Medical records and test reports from home country
- No medical condition that poses public health risk

### Duration

- 1-5 years depending on treatment
- Renewable if treatment extends
- Accompanying attendant gets Medical Attendant Visa

### Key Points

- Hospital must issue formal recommendation
- Can work or study but not engage in business
- Must follow treatment plan
- Processing faster than other visa types (often 2-3 weeks)

### Fees (2026)

- Similar to employment visa: ₹5,000-₹15,000

## 6. OCI (OVERSEAS CITIZEN OF INDIA)

### What Is It?

For people of Indian origin (ancestors who were Indian nationals) seeking long-term residency without citizenship.

### Eligibility

- You or your parents/grandparents were Indian nationals
- OR adopted by Indian citizens
- You're citizen of another country
- Not a citizen of Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, China (generally)

### Benefits

- 10-year visa validity
- Lifelong renewability
- Unrestricted travel to India
- Work/study without separate visa
- Property rights similar to Indian citizens
- No visa stamping required (document-based)

### Duration

- 10 years initially
- Renewable indefinitely
- Valid for lifetime residency

### Fees (2026)

- Initial: ₹21,000-₹35,000 (~$250-$420 USD)
- Renewal: ₹27,000-₹40,000

### Application Process

- Apply at Indian embassy/consulate
- Requires birth certificate, parent's documents, citizenship proof
- Processing: 4-8 weeks

## 7. TRANSIT VISA (SHORT STOPOVER)

### What Is It?

For passengers stopping over in India while traveling to another country. Usually valid for 3-15 days.

### Eligibility

- Onward ticket to third country
- Proof of visa for next destination
- No work intended

### Fees

- ₹1,000-2,000 typically

## STEP-BY-STEP: APPLYING FOR TOURIST e-VISA (MOST COMMON)

### Complete Walkthrough

**Before You Start:**
- Have passport handy
- Ensure you have:
  - Valid email address
  - Credit/debit card for payment
  - Digital photo (JPG, max 300KB)
  - Scanned passport page (color)

**Process (takes ~20 minutes):**

1. Visit https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/
2. Click "New visa application"
3. Select:
   - Country of passport
   - Visa type (30-day/1-year/5-year)
4. Fill personal details:
   - Full name (as in passport)
   - Date of birth
   - Passport number
   - Email address
   - Phone number
5. Answer security questions:
   - Have you visited India before?
   - Purpose of visit (tourism, business, etc.)
   - Intended entry date
   - Intended exit date
6. Upload documents:
   - Passport biographical page (scanned, color, clearly readable)
   - Recent photograph (JPG format, specific size - shown on form)
7. Review all information carefully
8. Pay visa fee (₹1050-₹5050 depending on type)
   - Visa/Mastercard accepted
   - Payment gateway is secure (look for HTTPS)
9. **Save application number** for reference
10. Submit application
11. Check email within 72 hours for approval
12. Once approved, **download and print e-Visa** (important)

**After Approval:**
- Print e-Visa on regular paper
- Keep copy with passport
- Bring to airport along with:
  - Original passport
  - Return ticket
  - Proof of accommodation
  - Proof of funds (bank statement)
  - Itinerary
- Show to immigration officer upon arrival

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Passport photo mistakes:**
- Using old, damaged, or colored photo
- Photo with filters/makeup altering features
- Incorrect background
- Incorrect dimensions

**Fix:** Use recent passport-quality photo, neutral background, clear face visible

❌ **Application timing:**
- Applying just days before travel
- Using expired passport

**Fix:** Apply 2-4 weeks before travel, ensure passport valid 6+ months

❌ **Incorrect passport data:**
- Spelling errors
- Mismatched information

**Fix:** Carefully verify all passport details before submitting

❌ **Missing documents:**
- Forgot to upload required file

**Fix:** Check requirements twice before final submission

❌ **Overstaying in India:**
- Staying longer than visa allows
- Not exiting before expiry date

**Fix:** Mark exit date in calendar, arrange departure well in advance

## FAQ: INDIA VISA QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I extend my tourist visa while in India?**
A: Difficult to extend tourist visas in India. Better to exit and re-enter if needed (if visa allows re-entry). Some extensions granted for genuine hardship—contact Foreigners Regional Registration Officer (FRRO).

**Q: Can I switch visa types once in India?**
A: Very limited. Student to work requires new application. Better to complete current visa purpose and reapply. Cannot switch tourist to employment while in India.

**Q: What if my visa application is rejected?**
A: Contact the embassy for specific reason. Most rejections due to document issues. Reapply after fixing issues. Appeals possible but uncommon.

**Q: Do I need travel insurance?**
A: Not officially required but highly recommended. Many insurance companies offer India-specific plans (₹500-2000 for 1 month).

**Q: Can I work on a tourist visa?**
A: No. Strictly forbidden. Violators face fines, deportation, future visa bans.

**Q: How long can I stay in India on a tourist visa?**
A: Max 90 days per entry (for most nationalities). Can be 180 days for some countries—check your specific rules on indianvisaonline portal.

**Q: What's difference between visa validity and stay duration?**
A: Visa validity = period visa is valid for entry. Stay duration = max days you can stay per entry. E.g., 1-year visa valid for 1 year but each stay max 90 days.

**Q: Do I need vaccinations to enter India?**
A: COVID-19 vaccination not required anymore (as of 2026). Yellow fever vaccine required only if traveling from endemic countries. Consult doctor for recommended vaccinations.

**Q: Can family members apply together?**
A: Yes, each applies separately but can coordinate. Requires individual applications and separate fees.

**Q: What if my visa expires while I'm in India?**
A: Serious matter. Report to FRRO immediately. Apply for extension or special permit. Violating overstay considered immigration offense.
