Bitcoin, created by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto in 2008, has grown from a niche technological experiment to a trillion-dollar asset class and the foundation of a new financial ecosystem.

## What Is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency that operates without a central authority or intermediary. Transactions are verified by network nodes through cryptography and recorded on a public distributed ledger called a blockchain.

## How It Works

Transactions are grouped into blocks. Miners compete to solve cryptographic puzzles, adding new blocks to the chain approximately every 10 minutes. Successful miners receive newly created bitcoin as a reward.

## Why Bitcoin Matters

**Financial Sovereignty**: Bitcoin gives individuals direct control over their money without needing banks or governments.

**Fixed Supply**: Only 21 million bitcoin will ever exist, making it inherently scarce like gold but more divisible and transferable.

**Global Transactions**: Send value anywhere in the world instantly, without intermediaries, for minimal fees.

**Censorship Resistance**: No single entity can block transactions or freeze accounts.

## Investment Considerations

Bitcoin has shown significant price volatility. Many financial advisors recommend only allocating a small percentage of a diversified portfolio.

## The Future

Bitcoin continues to evolve. The Lightning Network enables fast, cheap microtransactions. Institutional adoption is growing. Regulatory clarity is developing globally.
