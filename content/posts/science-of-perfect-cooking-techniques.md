Great cooking is about understanding the science that makes food delicious. Every cooking technique involves chemical reactions that transform ingredients.

## The Maillard Reaction

The Maillard reaction occurs when amino acids react with reducing sugars at temperatures above 285°F, creating hundreds of new flavor compounds and the golden-brown crust on seared steak, roasted chicken, and toasted bread.

## Heat Transfer

All cooking relies on transferring heat through conduction (direct contact), convection (air or liquid movement), or radiation (electromagnetic waves like a broiler).

## Emulsions: The Science of Sauces

An emulsion mixes two immiscible liquids like oil and water, stabilized by an emulsifier like egg yolks or mustard. Understanding emulsions transforms your ability to make sauces.

## The Chemistry of Baking

Baking requires precise ratios because chemical reactions must occur in specific proportions. Key reactions include leavening, gluten development, gelatinization, and protein coagulation.

## Temperature Control

Perhaps the most important cooking skill is temperature control. Every technique has an optimal temperature range.

## Food Safety

Understanding temperature danger zones and safe internal temperatures protects you and your family from foodborne illness.
