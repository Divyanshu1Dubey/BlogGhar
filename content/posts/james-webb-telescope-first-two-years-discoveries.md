The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST), launched on December 25, 2021, has revolutionized our understanding of the universe as the most powerful space telescope ever built.

## How Webb Differs From Hubble

Webb observes in infrared, allowing it to see through cosmic dust and detect light from the earliest galaxies. Its mirror is 6.5 meters in diameter, more than 2.5 times larger than Hubble's. It orbits at the second Lagrange point, 1.5 million km from Earth.

## Earliest Galaxies Ever Detected

Webb discovered surprisingly mature, bright, and massive galaxies just 300 million years after the Big Bang. Some are 10-100 times more massive than models predicted possible at that time.

## Exoplanet Atmospheres

Webb detected methane and carbon dioxide in the atmosphere of K2-18 b, a planet 120 light-years away in its star's habitable zone. It also detected sulfur dioxide in WASP-39 b's atmosphere — the first detection of photochemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere.

## Star Formation in Unprecedented Detail

Webb revealed hundreds of previously hidden stars in the Carina Nebula and detailed structures within the Pillars of Creation that were invisible to Hubble.

## Technical Achievements

Webb's deployment involved 344 single-point-of-failure steps. The successful mission represents one of the most complex engineering feats in space exploration history.
